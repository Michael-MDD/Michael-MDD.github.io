Terms of service

Application: SkyTower RFI archive
Updated: 8 November 2026

Authorized use

This workflow is intended for the archive owner and operators the owner explicitly authorizes. Use it only with accounts, project correspondence, files, and storage locations you are entitled to access and process.

Workflow behavior

The workflow reads Aconex correspondence and maintains archive copies, operational evidence, and a register in the configured Google Drive folder. Scheduled collection does not send correspondence or change business statuses in Aconex. Archive and register updates may be delayed or remain pending when an external service is unavailable or rejects requests.

Owner responsibilities

The owner controls account permissions, the configured destination, credential management, and access to project data. The owner is responsible for following applicable project and data-handling obligations and for reviewing reported failures or pending operations.

Availability and source records

The workflow depends on Aconex, Google, GitHub, and network availability. Continuous availability or immediate synchronization is not guaranteed. Aconex remains the source of the original correspondence. An archive copy does not replace the owner's contractual or project review responsibilities.

Ending use

The owner can disable scheduled jobs, remove workflow secrets, and revoke Google access. Existing Drive files remain until the owner deletes them.

Privacy and contact

Use of Google data is described in the Privacy policy. For support, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
